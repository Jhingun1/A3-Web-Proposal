Colour palette — “Golden-hour harbour”

Harbour Ink #0B1F3APrimary dark · nav, footer, headings
Harbour Blue #1B5FA8Primary interactive · links, buttons, selected states
Sunrise Coral #FF6B4AAccent only · CTAs, badges, highlights (≤10%)
Bondi Teal #2E8C8CSecondary accent · "free" & eco tags, charts
Sand #F4EDE3Background neutral · sections, cards on foam
How the palette was refined Analogous blues pulled from harbour water + a single warm coral from sunset ferries. Ink-on-sand body text = 13.9:1 contrast (WCAG AAA); coral-on-white = 3.4:1, so coral is restricted to large CTA text and icons where 3:1 passes. Tints of harbour blue (10/30/60) are the data-viz ramp.

Typography — editorial + neutral

Fraunces gives a premium, editorial travel feel — like a well-printed guidebook, not a template.

Why this pairing Serif display = heritage & culture; Inter = fast, practical planning. Target: “premium guidebook that plans for you.”
