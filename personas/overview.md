# Personas (quick overview for the pitch)

Three evidence-based personas, grounded in the visitor data (63.6M domestic / $20.8B; 8.9M int'l arrivals; 64% of international visitors are repeaters — TRA).

## 1. Jenna — the domestic first-timer (primary)
- **Who:** 27, graphic designer from Melbourne. First solo trip to Sydney, 2 nights, ~$1,200 budget.
- **Goals:** hit the icons, eat well, keep costs visible; book ferry/bridge climbs ahead.
- **Frustrations:** info scattered across destinations; can't tell what's free vs. ticketed; planning on her phone between meetings.
- **Design response:** experience-first home with cost/duration filters; "free days out" quick path; mobile sticky "add to plan"; itinerary share link to send to a friend.

## 2. David — the returning international visitor (secondary)
- **Who:** 41, product manager from Seattle, 4th visit, 10 nights, travelling with partner.
- **Goals:** beyond-the-icons — hidden beaches, local food scenes, evening culture; reliable transport info.
- **Frustrations:** same icons every time; can't find curated depth; weather uncertainty changes daily plans.
- **Design response:** "Go deeper" curated collections; day-by-day itinerary with swap suggestions by weather/time; events calendar; transport planner.

## 3. Aisha — the local re-explorer (tertiary)
- **Who:** 34, teacher living in Inner West, planning a free Saturday for her family.
- **Goals:** free/cheap, family-friendly, short travel time, no car.
- **Frustrations:** event listings buried; nothing filters by "free + this weekend."
- **Design response:** weekend/free filters; events section with time and cost; local-area grouping.

## What the analysis revealed
All three share: **time-poor, cost-aware, mobile-first planners** who want to *decide fast*. The decisive insight: Sydney's content should be organised around **experiences and constraints (time/cost)** rather than geography — which drove the whole IA, the home page hierarchy, and the mobile bottom-tab navigation.
